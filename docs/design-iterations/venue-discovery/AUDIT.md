# Discover venue integration

The original search returned normalized venue records to a modal; its only planning action reopened manual activity creation. The main deck remained generic. Venue details had useful provenance but no connection to persisted swipes or Undo.

Direction: extend the existing photo-first invitation. Put a concise search field above the existing deck, retain selected city and budget, reuse the exact swipe engine, and let results become private venue suggestions. Show title, place and two useful facts; disclose full address, hours, price uncertainty, suggested timing and source date in Details. No fictional attendees or scheduled sessions.

References inspected: existing real captures `docs/design-references/references/airbnb-search-mobile.png` (clear location/search hierarchy), `luma-event-mobile.png` (specific place and separate schedule), and `partiful-event-mobile.png` (photo-led identity and a clear interest action). Their event-attendance claims are not appropriate for a business listing.

Baseline: before-mobile.png and before-desktop.png. Impeccable context preserves the current identity. UI/UX Pro Max search guidance supplies visible labels, recovery actions, and announced errors. Narrow feature extension; no new design system or dependency.
