## Short project description

Ditto turns “we should hang out” into experiences people choose together. Friends chat in private Circles, submit ideas and vote independently. Meta Muse Spark extracts an editable proposal from selected messages and helps build an itinerary from real preferences, votes, budgets and constraints. SerpApi supplies actual local venues and driving information. Ditto keeps suggestions distinct from consensus: every participant approves the final plan and chooses whether to RSVP. AI removes coordination work while people retain the decisions.
