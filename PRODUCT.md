# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Friends choosing local activities together, often from a phone. Confirmed by the user on October 24, 2026. Desktop and MacBook interaction must be equally usable.

## Product Purpose

Move from independent interest to a collectively agreed outing, with an actionable plan.

## Positioning

Friends independently vote on the same activities; the group’s real votes determine consensus. AI assists planning but never invents agreement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve Discover, Circles, invitations, private voting rounds, matching, chat extraction, real venue discovery, Muse Spark planning, approvals, RSVP, routing, calendar export, authentication and persistent SQLite storage. Five-second polling remains. Physical right swipe means interested/DITTO, left means PASS. Keyboard and button alternatives remain. No production deployment without approval. Never expose credentials or private data.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Ditto, no trailing period. User-selected direction: Vibrant & Block-based + Motion-Driven. Original identity; Tinder interaction, Partiful personality, Airbnb discipline and Luma planning clarity are quality references, not assets to copy.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing working product, local tests, actual reference screenshots and source manifests under docs/ditto-design-references and docs/design-references. Authentic provider photos when available. Demo activities and stock imagery must remain distinguishable from real venues. No physical-device test evidence exists yet.

## Product Principles

- Real consensus, never fabricated social proof.
- One decisive activity at a time.
- Preserve context and recover safely from failed requests.
- Progressive disclosure without removing functionality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch, mouse and keyboard support. Preserve vertical scrolling, readable contrast, focus continuity, reduced-motion alternatives and phone safe areas.
