# Assets and dependencies

- Activity photographs: Unsplash sources and license links are recorded in `public/activities/manifest.json`. They illustrate activity categories, not verified venue photographs. See https://unsplash.com/license.
- Manrope and Syne fonts: Google Fonts source URLs are recorded in `public/fonts/manifest.json`; SIL Open Font License notices are included in `public/fonts/licenses/`.
- Official Ditto logo: supplied by the project owner. Its original design is preserved; the team should confirm its provenance and permission before publication. Original AI-generated onboarding artwork is identified in `public/brand/manifest.json`.
- Profile photos: supplied by the depicted team members through the project owner; stored locally in ignored databases/media, not bundled into application assets. Get each person's agreement before including their image in a public video.
- Third-party UI captures under design-reference documentation are historical research evidence. They are not shipped in the Docker app and are not licensed by this repository. Copyright remains with their respective owners. Public redistribution rights have not been established; review these captures (including Git history) before changing repository visibility.
- Next.js, React, Motion, Lucide, Zod, Tailwind and development tools retain their upstream licenses. Exact versions are locked in package-lock.json; no dependency was added for final submission readiness.
